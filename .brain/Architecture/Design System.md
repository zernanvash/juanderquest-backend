# JuanderQuest Design System (Figma & Stitch Reference)

**Figma Link:** `https://www.figma.com/proto/iMNm3VkAJBUous8NLoZHSj/JuanDerQuest?node-id=70-251`

## Color Tokens
- **Primary Gold:** `#FFB703` (Reward points, CTAs, highlight badges)
- **On-Primary:** `#6B4B00` (Dark contrast text on gold)
- **Secondary Green:** `#3F6653` (Category chips, tabs, eco branding)
- **Secondary Container:** `#BEEAD1` (Green badge containers)
- **Success Emerald:** `#2D6A4F` (Approved status, completed quests)
- **Wood Brown:** `#582F0E` (Headings, titles, brand typography)
- **Deep Ink:** `#0D1B2A` (Modal overlays, dark contrast text)
- **Warm Background:** `#FAF9F5` (Scaffold background)
- **Surface Container:** `#EFEEEA` (Input fields, step badges)
- **Outline Variant:** `#D5C4AC` (Borders, card dividers)
- **Error Red:** `#BC4749` (Rejection callouts, permission alerts)

## Typography Pairings
- **Headings:** Epilogue (700 Bold / 800 ExtraBold)
- **Body & Controls:** Plus Jakarta Sans (400 Regular / 600 SemiBold / 700 Bold)

## UI Components
- **Top Bar:** Brand logo + `1,250 PTS` token pill
- **Category Chips:** `All Quests`, `Eco`, `Cultural`, `Food & Trade`
- **Quest Cards:** Featured Hero Card (gradient overlay + reward badge) & Standard Quest Row Cards
- **Status Badges:** `Awaiting administrator review`, `Quest approved — +50 points awarded`, `Proof rejected`
